Hi, my name is Sydney Gibson, and I am from Cincinnati, Ohio. I was born with Cerebral Palsy, but that did not stop me from achieving my dreams. Growing up, I have learned to be strong, determined, and not let challenges stop me from doing the things I want to accomplish.

In my spare time, I love to read, color, and play video games . One of my favorite video games is Call of Duty. I enjoy playing video games because they are fun and give me a way to relax and have a good time. I also play my games using one hand, which is something I have learned to do and enjoy.

I work at a nonprofit organization called LADD. LADD stands for Living Arrangements for the Developmental Disablities. They helps people with developmental disabilities find housing, jobs, and become more involved in their communities. I work there as an office assistant, and I enjoy being part of an organization that helps people and makes a difference in their lives.

I am currently majoring in Communication at the University of Cincinnati. I am excited to continue my education and learn new skills that can help me reach my future goals. My experiences have taught me that having a disability does not mean I cannot achieve my dreams. I want to continue growing, learning, and showing others that anything is possible when you believe in yourself and do not give up.
